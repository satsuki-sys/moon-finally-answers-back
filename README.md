## What it does

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) writes one original line,
in the voice of the Moon replying at last to the wishes, howls and
late-night promises of everyone staring up at it, to `/output/entry.txt`.

## The line

> You wish on me, howl at me, and swear you'll change tomorrow. Four billion years of it, and nobody has once asked how I'm doing. I'm fine. The tides are still on. Your basement, Kevin — that was your idea, not mine.

215 characters, one line. The Moon answers the wishers directly, sounds
wry and old and a little tired of being wished upon, and the turn lands
at the end: the flooding everyone blames on the tides was Kevin's own
building decision.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Then read `/output/entry.txt`.
